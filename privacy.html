Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

What we collect

  • At payment (via Razorpay): name, mobile number, email and payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled only by Razorpay.
  • On Telegram: your Telegram name, username and user ID, and the mobile number or email you send to our bot for verification.

How we use it

Only to verify your payment, give and manage your membership access (including removing access when it expires), send renewal or service messages, and answer your queries.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the service (Razorpay for payments, Telegram for delivery) or when required by law.

Retention and security

Membership records are kept for the membership period and up to 12 months after, for accounting and support, then deleted. Records are stored on access-controlled systems.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to baren.sahoo@gmail.com.
