Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The ASO Premium Membership is a digital product delivered immediately after payment verification. Because content becomes accessible at once, payments are generally non-refundable. Refunds are given in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment – the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • No access – if we cannot give you access to the Premium channel within 48 hours of a successful payment, you get a full refund on request.
  • Service discontinued – if we stop the service before your validity ends, a pro-rata refund is given for the remaining period.

Cancellation

You may cancel within 24 hours of payment if you have not yet joined the Premium channel; a full refund is given. After joining, the membership cannot be cancelled.

How to request

Write to baren.sahoo@gmail.com or +91 76090 08060 with your Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_). Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
